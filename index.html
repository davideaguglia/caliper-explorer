World caliper market by year

Summary by year

“Incumbents” = Western, Japanese and Korean suppliers. Chinese = Chinese-owned (BWI follows the classification lever above).

Method. 2025 light-vehicle production for ~20 OEM groups (≈85% of the market) plus regional residuals, rounded approximations to be replaced with OICA / S&P data. Calipers per car from regional rear-drum shares. Each OEM-region row is split across suppliers; suppliers are grouped by controlling owner (CBI is counted inside Astemo). OE light vehicles only: no trucks, no aftermarket.

Base case. ≈335M calipers a year for ≈92M light vehicles (≈3.6 per vehicle, below 4 because of rear drum brakes). All shares are by units.
